# Privacy

FocusBreak is designed as a local-first macOS utility.

## Data Collection

FocusBreak does not collect analytics, tracking data, personal information, or usage history.

## Local Settings

Break preferences, counters, and launch-at-login preferences are stored locally on your Mac using standard macOS storage.

## Network

FocusBreak does not require an account and does not send your break settings or usage data to a server.

## Notifications

If notifications are enabled, FocusBreak uses local macOS notifications to remind you to take breaks.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in this repository.
